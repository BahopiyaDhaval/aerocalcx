This website is developed as a simple engineering calculator for aerodynamic and gasdynamic applications. It helps users calculate Mach Number, Dynamic Pressure, Reynolds Number, and Speed of Sound.

Formulas Used

  • Mach Number: M = V / a
  • Dynamic Pressure: q = 0.5 × ρ × V²
  • Reynolds Number: Re = (ρ × V × L) / μ
  • Speed of Sound: a = √(γ × R × T)
